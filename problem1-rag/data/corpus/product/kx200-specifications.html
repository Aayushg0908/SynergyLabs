KX-200 Survey Drone — Technical Specifications

The KX-200 is Kestrel Labs' quadcopter for agricultural and infrastructure surveying. It carries interchangeable sensor payloads and streams telemetry through a Vela gateway or directly over LTE.

Battery and endurance

The KX-200 uses the KB-22 smart battery, a 22,000 mAh lithium-ion pack. Flight time is 42 minutes with no payload and 31 minutes with the maximum 1.5 kg payload, measured at sea level in calm conditions. A full charge with the standard charger takes 75 minutes; the fast charger reduces this to 45 minutes.

KB-22 batteries are rated for 400 charge cycles before capacity falls below 80%. The drone refuses to arm if the battery reports more than 600 cycles.

Navigation and communication

  • GNSS: GPS, GLONASS, Galileo and BeiDou, with optional RTK module for 2 cm horizontal accuracy.
  • Obstacle sensing: forward, rear and downward stereo vision, effective range 0.5–30 m.
  • Control link range: up to 8 km (FCC) or 5 km (CE) line of sight.
  • Telemetry: 10 Hz position and health telemetry; payload data uploaded after landing or streamed if bandwidth allows.
