Vela Field Gateway

The Vela gateway is a ruggedized edge device that collects telemetry from up to 16 KX-series drones at once and forwards it to Kestrel Cloud. It is designed for remote farms and construction sites with poor connectivity.

Connectivity

  • Uplink: LTE Cat-12, Ethernet, or optional satellite modem (Iridium Certus).
  • Drone link: 900 MHz long-range radio, range up to 10 km with the high-gain antenna.
  • Automatic failover between uplinks in under 5 seconds.

Local buffering

When the uplink is lost, Vela stores telemetry on its internal 256 GB SSD. The buffer holds up to 6 hours of telemetry from a full fleet of 16 drones. When the buffer is full, the oldest data is overwritten first. Buffered data is uploaded automatically and in order once the uplink returns.

Security

All traffic between Vela and Kestrel Cloud is encrypted with TLS 1.3 using mutual certificate authentication. Device certificates rotate automatically every 90 days. Firmware images are signed and verified at boot.

Pricing and subscription

The Vela gateway costs $3,200. A Kestrel Cloud connectivity subscription is required: $45 per gateway per month, which includes 50 GB of LTE data. Additional LTE data is billed at $6 per GB.
