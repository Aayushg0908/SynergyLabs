Warranty and Repairs

Standard limited warranty

KX-200 drones and Vela gateways are covered by a 24-month limited warranty from the date of delivery. KB-22 batteries are covered for 12 months or 200 charge cycles, whichever comes first. Payloads (MS-5, TH-1 and LX-40) are covered for 12 months.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and component failures under normal use. It does not cover crash damage, water damage beyond the device's IP rating, damage from unauthorized modifications, or normal wear of propellers.

Kestrel Care+

Kestrel Care+ extends the warranty to 36 months and adds accidental-damage coverage, including crashes. Care+ costs $1,490 per drone per year and must be purchased within 30 days of delivery. Each Care+ plan includes up to two accidental-damage replacements per year, each subject to a $350 service fee.

How to request a repair (RMA)

  1. Open a support ticket with the device serial number and a description of the issue.
  2. Upload the flight logs for the last 3 flights; support cannot triage crashes without them.
  3. Support issues a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number, normally within 1 business day.
  4. Ship the device using the prepaid label. Devices shipped without an RMA number are returned unopened.

Standard repair turnaround is 10 business days from receipt. Enterprise customers receive an advance replacement unit shipped within 2 business days, before the faulty unit is returned.

Out-of-warranty repairs

Out-of-warranty repairs are quoted after inspection. The inspection fee is $150, which is waived if you go ahead with the repair.
